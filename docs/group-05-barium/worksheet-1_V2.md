## 2. Observe the Current Process

### 1. What process did you observe?

> The process of students keeping track of their academic assessments, including assignments, quizzes, projects, activities, deadlines, and other requirements across different subjects.

<br>

### 2. What is the purpose of this process?

> To help students organize and monitor their academic requirements so they can remember important deadlines, identify urgent assessments, and complete their tasks on time.

<br>

### 3. Who are involved in the process?

**(Up to two roles only.)**

| Person/Role | Responsibility in the Process |
| ----------- | ----------------------------- |
| 1. Students | 	Record, check, prioritize, and complete their assessments based on their deadlines and importance.                             |
| 2. Teachers | 	Provide assessment information such as the task name, subject, requirements, deadline, and importance.                             |

### 4. What are the steps in the current process?

| Step | What happens? | Person/Role Involved |
| ---- | ------------- | -------------------- |
| 1    | 	Teachers announce assignments, quizzes, projects, and other assessments through different platforms or during class. |  Teachers  |
| 2    | 	Students receive the assessment information and manually record or remember the task and its deadline. | Students |
| 3    | 	Students check different sources such as messages, classroom platforms, notes, and calendars to find their upcoming requirements. |  Students |
| 4    | 	Students determine which assessments need to be completed first based on their deadlines, importance, and workload. | Students |
| 5    | 	Students complete the assessments and keep track of which requirements are finished or still pending.| Students |

## 3. Analyze the Problem

### 1. What problems or difficulties did you observe?

> Students may have difficulty keeping track of assessments when deadlines, requirements, and announcements are spread across different subjects and platforms. Without a centralized system, students can forget deadlines, overlook important assessments, or spend too much time deciding which task to prioritize. Multiple assessments with similar deadlines can also make it difficult to manage their workload effectively.

<br>
